	{\large Author's biography}
\end{center}

{\bf Omar Lizardo} is an Assistant Professor of sociology at the University of Notre Dame.  His research interests include the sociology of culture and taste, cognitive sociology, social networks, institutional theory, social theory and the sociology of globalization.  His most recent research deals with the effect of different forms of cultural engagement on network closure (\emph{Sociological Perspectives}, 2011) and the effect of status position at work on patterns of cultural engagement (\emph{Sociological Inquiry}, forthcoming).
